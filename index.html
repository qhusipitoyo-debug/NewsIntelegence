Gold watch

XAU/USD

Live gold levels from the active USD catalyst. Before the release, BUY/SELL comes from the Forecast-vs-Previous bias. After it, entries wait 15 minutes. On the SignalEngine path, Actual and the 15-minute price reaction must agree (conflict = no trade); the older reaction → momentum → Actual ladder applies only to the legacy path. Confirmed Entry/SL/TP are locked so they do not repaint; Straddle OCO stays as the unmeasured fallback.

Crypto watch

BTC/USD & ETH/USD

Always-on crypto prices, independent of the event currency. Same ladder as the other boards: wait 15 minutes after the release, then Actual must agree with the price reaction on the engine path. Confirmed Entry/SL/TP are locked. TP2 sits 2R away, but realised R is ~0.5R at TP1/BE or ~1.5R at TP2 — not 2R on every win.

Forex watch

Forex Majors & JPY Crosses

Pre-news market entry bias for EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY, USD/CHF, AUD/USD, USD/CAD, NZD/USD, GBP/JPY and EUR/JPY. Prices use the live OANDA feed (TradingView) with a multi-broker cross-check. When the local MT5 bridge is running, the exact broker bid/ask is shown and used for the entry side (ask for BUY, bid for SELL). After a release, entries wait 15 minutes and the confirmed Entry/SL/TP are locked. It is a scenario, not a prediction.

Arah Pasar HTF — Final Hanya Saat D/W/M Align

Setiap aset dibaca dari bar harian Yahoo (1d, 5 tahun) yang diagregasi ke daily/weekly/monthly. Final keluar hanya bila D+W+M sepakat, atau D+W sepakat dengan Monthly netral — selama 15 menit pasca-rilis final dikunci WAIT. Target per-TF adalah ilustrasi; level eksekusi memakai ATR daily dengan entry bid/ask live.

• Ambang Kejutan (satuan makro): konstanta per indikator dalam satuan rilis (pp / jobs / poin), berasal dari distribusi surprise historis — bukan dari ATR 1 menit.
• Noise Mikro (satuan harga): ATR 1m/5m dipakai hanya sebagai ambang reaksi harga pasca-rilis dan filter volatilitas; kedua skala ini tidak dicampur.
• Order Execution Bertingkat: Entry Pasar / Breakout + Level Pullback (Retest OTE) untuk rasio RR terbaik.
• Target Bertingkat (definisi win tunggal = R > 0): TP1 1R tutup 50% lalu SL ke BE → realisasi ~0.5R; TP2 2R untuk sisa 50% → realisasi ~1.5R. R diukur dari entry yang sudah termasuk spread (tidak dikurangi biaya dua kali).
• Filter Spread & Spike: Delay konfirmasi 15 menit pasca-rilis mencegah terjebak di spike pertama.
• Prioritas Arah: Reaksi Harga 1M > Momentum Terkalibrasi > Actual Fundamental vs Forecast.
